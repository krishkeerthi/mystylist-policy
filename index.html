MyStylist

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 July 2026  ·  Last updated: 8 July 2026

In plain language: MyStylist stores all your wardrobe data and photos on your device only. No account is needed. We do not sell your data. The only time data leaves your device is when you add a clothing item (photo sent to Google Gemini AI for tagging) or ask for outfit suggestions (text sent to Google Gemini AI). Google does not use these requests to train its models under the current API terms.

1. Who We Are

MyStylist ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a personal AI wardrobe and outfit-planning application for Android, developed and published by Keerthi K (keerthik97117@gmail.com). This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use it.

By installing and using MyStylist, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Information We Collect

The App collects two types of information: data you provide directly, and data generated automatically by the App.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Clothing photos – taken with your camera or picked from your gallery. Photos are processed on your device (background removal) and stored in the App's private internal storage. They are not accessible to other apps.
  • Wardrobe item details – name, category, colour, fabric, formality, season, occasions, gender, and status for each clothing item you add.
  • Profile information – your display name, gender preference, home city, and style preferences (Minimal, Classic, Trendy, etc.). These are stored locally only.
  • Outfit requests – the text you type into the Stylist screen (e.g. "5-day trip to the hills"). This text is sent to Google Gemini AI to generate outfit suggestions.

2.2 Information Generated Automatically

  • Wear count – the App tracks how many times each item has been included in a suggested outfit. Stored locally only.
  • Item status – Available, In Laundry, or Packed. Stored locally only.

The App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (no account is required)
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IMEI numbers
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics
  • Contacts, calendar, or any data outside the App's own storage

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to operate the App:

  • To display your wardrobe items and photos within the App.
  • To auto-tag clothing items using Google Gemini AI (photo is sent to Google's API, a structured tag result is returned).
  • To generate personalised day-by-day outfit plans using Google Gemini AI (your wardrobe list, profile, and request text are sent to Google's API).
  • To provide an offline outfit engine (all processing done on-device, no network required).
  • To track wear counts and item status to surface less-worn items in outfit suggestions.

We do not use your information for:

  • Advertising or marketing
  • Selling to or sharing with data brokers or advertisers
  • Building user profiles for external purposes
  • Any purpose beyond operating the features described above

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google Gemini API (Google AI Studio)

MyStylist uses the Google Gemini API (via Firebase AI Logic) to power two features: AI photo tagging and outfit suggestion generation. When you add a clothing item or request outfit suggestions, the relevant data (photo and/or text) is sent to Google's servers over HTTPS.

Under the Google AI Studio Developer API terms (free tier), Google states that it does not use API inputs and outputs to train or improve its generative models. Requests are processed and the result is returned; Google does not retain your data beyond the processing window. See Google's Gemini API Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for full details.

5.2 Firebase App Check (Google)

The App uses Firebase App Check with Play Integrity to verify that API requests come from the genuine MyStylist app and not from unauthorised sources. This sends a device integrity attestation to Google. No personal data is included in this attestation. See Firebase Privacy Information.

5.3 Google ML Kit (On-Device)

Background removal from clothing photos is performed entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit Subject Segmentation library. No photo data is sent to Google's servers for this feature. Processing happens locally and the result stays on your device.

5.4 No Analytics, No Ads

The App does not include any advertising SDK (Google AdMob, Meta Ads, etc.), analytics SDK (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), or crash reporting service (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.).

7. Data Storage and Security

All wardrobe data (item details, wear counts, favourites) is stored in a SQLite database in the App's private internal storage using Android's Room library. Clothing photos are stored in the App's private internal directory. Both locations are:

  • Not accessible to other apps on your device (Android sandbox)
  • Deleted automatically when you uninstall the App
  • Eligible for Android's standard device encryption if you have enabled a screen lock

We do not operate any servers or cloud databases. We have no ability to access your wardrobe data remotely.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the App. You can delete individual wardrobe items at any time from within the App. To delete all data, uninstall the App — this permanently removes all photos, item records, and profile information from your device.

Because we do not operate a server or maintain a copy of your data, there is no additional deletion step required on our side.

11. Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device and we do not hold a copy, you already have full control:

  • Access – All your data is visible within the App.
  • Correction – Edit any item from the Edit Item screen.
  • Deletion – Delete individual items within the App, or uninstall to delete everything.
  • Portability – Your data is in a SQLite database on your own device (path: App internal storage). Android backup exports are supported.

For any questions about how Google processes data sent to its Gemini API, you may exercise your rights under Google's privacy framework at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.
